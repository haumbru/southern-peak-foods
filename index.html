Real ingredients, big clusters

Most protein cereal tastes like it came out of a tub. We bake oats, nuts and seeds into thick, crunchy clusters first, then add protein. The result tastes like granola, not a supplement.

It is made for people who train, so the protein number is on the front of the pouch where you can see it.

  • Rolled oatsThe base of every cluster
  • Hazelnuts and almondsRoasted for crunch
  • Pumpkin seedsMore texture and minerals
  • CocoaRich flavour without piles of sugar
